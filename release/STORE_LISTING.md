## Full description

One Kural. One reflection. One small action.

Make room for a little Tamil wisdom in your day. Nitham Oru Kural helps you read a Thirukkural, reflect on its meaning, and consider a small way to put the idea into practice in everyday life or work.

CONNECT READING WITH EVERYDAY LIFE
Explore practical examples, life and work application prompts, and a small suggested action. Use them as starting points for your own reflection.

CHOOSE WHAT MATTERS TO YOU
Select from nine interests, including learning, communication, relationships, leadership and time management. Your daily reading is chosen from your selected topics.

BUILD A DAILY READING ROUTINE
Keep your daily reading focused in Basic Mode. Set an optional reminder, mark a Kural as read, and see your consecutive reading-day streak.

KEEP YOUR OWN REFLECTIONS
Save favorite Kurals and write a private note for each one. Your notes and preferences stay on your device.

EXPLORE THE COMPLETE COLLECTION OFFLINE
All 1,330 Kurals and 133 chapters are included in the app. Switch on Advanced Mode to search by number, word, topic or chapter. Reading works without an internet connection.

READ IN A WAY THAT SUITS YOU
Choose Tamil, Tanglish or English reading modes. Phonetic Tanglish provides an additional reading aid.

ENJOY A QUIET READING SPACE
No account registration, advertisements or analytics trackers. The native Android interface supports a focused reading experience.

SHARE A THOUGHT
Share a Kural as text or a generated image through an app you choose. Private reflection notes are not included in Kural sharing.

GOOD TO KNOW
Practical examples are topic-based reflection prompts. Voice reading requires a suitable locally installed offline voice. Android permissions and battery settings can delay reminders. Sharing through another app may require internet access. Notes and favorites are stored on this device; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Start with one Kural today, and give its meaning a moment in your own life.
