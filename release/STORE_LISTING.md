## Full description

Build a daily reading habit with Nitham Oru Kural.

Explore all 1,330 Thirukkural verses across 133 chapters, with Tamil text, phonetic readings, and English translations. Choose Tamil, Tanglish, or English explanations and reflect on practical applications for everyday life and work.

• Read the full collection offline, including on your first launch after installation.
• Start with a focused daily reading or browse the complete directory in Advanced Mode.
• Choose interests such as learning, ethics, leadership, relationships, and communication.
• Set an optional daily reminder on Android.
• Save favorite Kurals and write private reflection notes on your device.
• Track completed reading days and your current daily streak.
• Share selected Kural text or an image using your favorite apps.

No account, advertisements, analytics trackers, or background AI processing.

Practical examples are topic-based reflection prompts. Tanglish is a phonetic aid. Voice reading, where supported, requires a locally installed device voice. Reminder delivery depends on Android permissions and battery settings. Sharing through another app may require an internet connection.

Your favorites and notes stay on this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
