Privacy Policy — Nitham Oru Kural

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Developer: Smartiva

Android package: com.nithamorukural.habitapp

Contact: info@smartiva.in

Nitham Oru Kural is an offline Thirukkural reading and daily reflection app provided by Smartiva. This policy explains how the app handles information and device permissions.

1. Information we collect

The Android app does not collect or send personal information, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, or your reading history to Smartiva servers. It has no account registration, advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or background AI services.

All 1,330 Kurals and the reading content are included in the app. Reading does not require an internet connection.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores language preferences, selected topics, reminder settings, favorites, reading completion dates, and personal reflection notes locally on your device. The app does not upload or synchronize this information to a server.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the Android app removes these saved preferences and notes. The Android app disables Android backup for its app data. The app does not provide cloud backup or recovery.

3. Optional notification permission

If you enable daily reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your Android device. Notifications do not use a remote push service. You can disable reminders in the app or revoke notification permission in Android settings. Delivery may be delayed by device battery settings or restrictions. Browser reminders require the web app to remain open.

4. Sharing and external apps

Sharing is optional and starts only when you choose a sharing action. The selected Kural text or image is passed to the app or service you choose, such as WhatsApp. Your private reflection notes are not included in Kural sharing. The recipient app or service handles the content according to its own privacy policy. External services may require an internet connection.

Images generated for Android sharing may be stored temporarily in the app's cache. Android or the user can clear this cache.

5. Optional voice reading

Where supported, voice reading uses locally available device voices. Voice availability depends on your browser, device, and installed voice packs. The app does not send text or audio to Smartiva for speech processing.

6. Website and web version

The Android reading app works offline. If you visit our website, this privacy-policy page, or the web version, your browser contacts the relevant hosting provider to download files. Hosting providers may process ordinary connection information, such as IP address and request logs, according to their own policies. Website operation and any other Smartiva services may be covered by separate privacy notices.

The web version stores preferences and notes in browser local storage and uses a service worker cache to support offline reading after the first successful load. Clearing browser site data removes these saved items.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require an account or ask for a child's name, age, email address, or location. If you have a concern about a child's use of the app, contact us at info@smartiva.in.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You can edit or remove personal notes, remove favorites, and change reminder settings in the app. To remove all saved app information, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. For the web version, clear the site's browser data. There is no server account to delete.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will display a revised effective date. Any future feature that changes data handling will require an updated policy and applicable permission or consent.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Smartiva at info@smartiva.in.
