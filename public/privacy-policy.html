100% Offline · Zero Data Collection

Privacy Policy — Nitham Oru Kural (நித்தம் ஒரு குறள்)

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Nitham Oru Kural ("the Application") is a 100% offline daily Thirukkural habit-building application designed for Android and Web platforms. We are committed to protecting your privacy completely.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

The Application operates 100% offline on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, or usage analytics on any external servers.

2. Local On-Device Storage Only

  • Your chosen Daily Schedule Time, Interest Topics, Language Preference (Simple Tamil, Tanglish, or English), Saved Favorites, Daily Habit Streak, and Personal Reflection Notes are stored strictly inside your device's local storage (localStorage / on-device cache).
  • This data never leaves your phone. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the application permanently deletes all locally stored preferences and notes.

3. No Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Background Cloud Processing

All 1,330 Thirukkural couplets, explanations, real-world examples, and life/business applications are pre-bundled directly inside the application package. No background AI processing or external API requests are executed.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (Optional): Used solely on your device to display your daily scheduled Thirukkural habit reminder at the time you configure.

5. Third-Party Services & Children's Privacy

The Application contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no user accounts. It is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13 (COPPA compliant).

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our Google Play Store listing, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
